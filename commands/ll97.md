Calculate Local Law 97 compliance for this property:

1. Identify occupancy group and GFA
2. Look up the 2024-2029 carbon threshold for this occupancy type
3. Calculate current carbon emissions (from benchmarking data or uploaded utility bills)
4. Compute the compliance gap: emissions vs. threshold
5. Calculate annual LL97 penalty if non-compliant
6. Project 2030-2034 exposure (stricter thresholds)
7. Recommend top 3 actions to close the gap with estimated cost
